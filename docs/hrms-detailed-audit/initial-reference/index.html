Workflows described by the guide

Apply leave → Pending → one HR/Admin approve/reject decision → employee notification → balance/calendar update.

Approved leave → employee requests cancellation before leave ends → HR/Admin approves cancellation → Cancelled → balance restored.

Guide claims email and WhatsApp alerts on submission, and email, WhatsApp and in-app alerts on decision. Confirm delivery in a test environment.

Policies to confirm: one paid day/month with indefinite carry-forward; Monday/Friday restrictions; unlimited unpaid permission leave; department-specific weekends and holiday exclusions. Filters and calendar show additional leave types not present in the application form.
